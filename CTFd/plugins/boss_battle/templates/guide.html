📜 Club Rules & Code of Conduct

  • 🔹 Fair Play: Do not share flags, hints, or solutions with other teams.
  • 🔹 Infrastructure: Do not perform denial-of-service (DoS) or attack platform infrastructure.
  • 🔹 Flag Format: Flags are formatted as CTF{...} unless specified otherwise in the challenge description.
  • 🔹 Need Help? Reach out to club organizers / mentors on Discord or the support desk.

👾 How the Boss Battle Works

  • 💥 Solves Deal Damage: Every successful challenge solve deals damage directly to the global boss!
  • 🔄 3 Progressive Phases: Deplete each phase's HP to unlock new animations and harder boss stages.
  • 📊 Live Widget: Watch the boss HP bar update live on any page via the bottom-right widget.
  • 🏆 Last Hit Attribution: Solve strategically to land the finishing blow on each phase!
